Software Engineer, New Grad — Stripe
San Francisco, CA (Hybrid)

About the role:
We're looking for new grad software engineers to join our team building the
infrastructure that powers internet commerce. You'll work on distributed systems
that process millions of API requests per second, build developer tools, and
help design the next generation of financial infrastructure.

What you'll do:
- Design, build, and maintain APIs, services, and systems across Stripe's engineering teams
- Debug production issues across services and levels of the stack
- Work with engineers across the company to build new features at large scale
- Improve engineering standards, tooling, and processes

Who you are:
- You have a BS/MS in Computer Science or equivalent experience
- You have strong programming skills (we primarily use Ruby, Java, Python, and Go)
- You have experience with distributed systems, databases, or infrastructure
- You are comfortable working in a Unix/Linux environment
- You write clean, maintainable code and care about software design
- You have strong communication skills and enjoy collaborating

Nice to have:
- Experience with AWS, GCP, or other cloud platforms
- Familiarity with Docker, Kubernetes, or container orchestration
- Experience with CI/CD pipelines and infrastructure as code
- Contributions to open source projects
- Experience building and consuming REST APIs

Compensation: $180,000 - $220,000 base salary + equity + benefits
